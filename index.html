▶ HOW WE EDIT

Everything your calls turn into, and how long it takes. Read it once and you'll know what to expect.

◆ THE SIX CLIP TYPES ◆

Every clip we cut is one of these. Most calls give us three to five. We never post five of the same kind — you get a spread.

🎣
OPENER
How you get them to stay on the phone. The first few lines that stop a hang-up.
🔍
DISCOVERY
The question that gets them talking about why they really picked up.
🛡️
OBJECTION
They push back, you handle it. Always starts with them saying it, so the answer makes sense.
🏁
CLOSE
Asking for the business, and what you do in the silence afterwards.
🤝
RAPPORT
The moment they stop treating you like a salesperson.
😂
FUNNY RARE
A call going sideways in a way other agents will recognise. Most calls have none — that's normal.

Clips run as long as the moment needs — a sharp line might be 20 seconds, a whole objection handled start to finish might be 90. You also get your full call video with the clean phone-call audio dropped in, so you can cut your own if you want. Every clip comes with a one-page clip sheet: a thumbnail, a caption ready to copy and paste, and a plain-English note on why it was cut that way.

◆ HOW LONG IT TAKES ◆
UPLOADING
~8 min per GB
EDITING
about as long as the call

Uploading depends on your wifi, not on us — a 2 GB video is usually around a quarter of an hour. Keep the page open while it goes up. Editing depends on how long the call was, not how big the file is: a 15-minute call takes us about 12 minutes, a 30-minute call about 25. You get an email the moment it's done, with a link straight to that call's folder.

◆ WHAT WE DO AUTOMATICALLY ◆
  • Cut the ums and uhs — and the hedging "like", so your point lands faster.
  • Tighten dead air — but we keep the pause after a question. That silence is doing work.
  • Bleep the client's name the first time, cut it every time after.
  • Strip anything compliance won't allow — carrier names, locations, money details, contact details.
  • Burn in captions from your actual words, so it reads with the sound off.
  • Check it twice — anything still questionable gets dropped rather than sent to you.
◆ GET A HIGHER SCORE ◆
  • Wear headphones. Then we can tell your voice from theirs — that's what makes the zoom work.
  • Start filming when you dial. Anything recorded before the call starts can't be matched up or used.
  • Film only yourself. No screens, quote tools, CRM or paperwork in the shot.
  • Name your call on the upload screen. That name becomes the folder in your Drive.
  • Flip the zoom switch on. The camera pushes in while you talk and pulls back for the client. It holds attention.
  • Save your caption style once on My Style — clicking a preset only previews it.
